Realms of the Ring: privacy

Realms of the Ring is a seated, hands-only WebXR game by Bloomind Studio for Meta Quest 3. This page says what the game does with data. Last updated: 18 November 2026.

In short

The game runs entirely in your browser. It does not collect, send or sell any personal information. It does not use the microphone or the camera.

What the game does on your device

While you play, the browser reads your hand and head positions from the headset. They are used on the device to play the game. They are not recorded and not sent anywhere.

The game loads its own pages, models, voices and sounds from the address you opened it from. AEON, the in-game guide, speaks from lines recorded in advance and stored with the game.

What is stored on your device

The game saves your progress (the story step you reached) in your browser's local storage, so that a reload can continue where you were. It may also save a small display setting, and while you play it may keep anonymous frame-timing numbers for the current browser session. All of this stays on your device and is not sent to us. To remove it, clear this site's data in your browser.

What we do not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No ads and no analytics.
  • No tracking cookies.
  • No microphone, no camera, no recordings.
  • Nothing is collected from children or from anyone else.
